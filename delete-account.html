Account & Data Deletion Portal

Compliance: Google Play Data Safety & User Privacy Policy • App: Chess Openings: Trainer & AI (com.chessopenings.trainer) • Developer: ROHITH WILSON T

In compliance with Google Play User Data policies, users of Chess Openings: Trainer & AI may request the complete and permanent deletion of their account and all associated cloud data.

What Data Gets Permanently Deleted?

Initiating an account deletion permanently purges the following records from our systems:

  • Authentication Profile: Your registered email address, authentication credentials, and user identifier in Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Cloud Match & Review History: All recorded games, Stockfish analysis results, blunder reviews, and key moments stored in Google Cloud Firestore (/gameHistory/{uid}).
  • Opening Mastery & Study Plans: Your opening repertoire progress, spaced-repetition schedules, learning liabilities, and tactical study plans (/users/{uid} and /progress/{uid}).

Retention & Disaster Recovery: Active cloud data is purged immediately upon verification. Encrypted disaster-recovery backups are purged within standard 30–60 day lifecycle overwrites. Offline local storage on your physical device can also be cleared via Android Settings > Apps > Chess Openings > Clear Storage.

Option 2: Delete Directly Inside the Mobile App

If you currently have the mobile app installed, you can delete your account instantly without using this web portal:

  1. Open Chess Openings: Trainer & AI on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Under the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm and permanently delete your account and local device data.

Option 3: Manual Deletion via Support Email

If you signed in with an external provider, forgot your password, or require manual support, you can submit an email deletion request directly to our developer team:

Send an email from your registered account address with the subject line "Data Deletion Request":

gamewingssupport@gmail.com

Manual deletion requests are processed and confirmed within 7 business days following identity verification.
