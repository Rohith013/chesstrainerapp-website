Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • App: Chess Openings: Trainer & AI (com.chessopenings.trainer) • Developer: ROHITH WILSON T

This Privacy Policy explains how ROHITH WILSON T ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application Chess Openings: Trainer & AI (referred to as the "App").

Quick Summary: We respect your privacy. You can use all core features of the App as a Guest without providing any personal information. If you create an account, we use Google Firebase strictly to sync your chess match history and settings. We do not sell your personal data or display intrusive advertisements.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information (Optional)

The App allows you to play and train completely anonymously as a Guest. If you choose to register an account or sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect:

  • Email Address: To identify your user account and authenticate your sign-in.
  • Display Name: To personalize your in-app profile.

B. Chess Training & Game Data

To provide opening mastery tracking and personal progress analytics, the App saves:

  • Match history, moves played, match outcomes, and blunder reviews.
  • Opening repertoire mastery levels, quiz results, and spaced-repetition schedules.
  • Tactical puzzle ratings and completed puzzle indices.
  • App preferences (dark mode, sound effects, language selection).

This data is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB/local storage. If you sign in, it is synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore.

C. Diagnostic & Analytics Information

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage metrics, including:

  • Screen views and feature interactions (e.g., puzzles solved, games analyzed).
  • App launch times, session duration, and device model/operating system version.
  • Non-fatal errors and crash logs to help us identify and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing Core Services: Enabling you to train openings, play against the Stockfish engine, and solve chess puzzles.
  • Cloud Synchronization: Restoring your match history and ratings across multiple devices when logged in.
  • App Quality & Improvement: Diagnosing performance bottlenecks, fixing crashes, and improving user experience.
  • Customer Support: Responding to user inquiries and troubleshooting technical issues.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

The App utilizes trusted Google Cloud / Firebase services that adhere to stringent security and privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Handles secure sign-in and account credentials.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud database for syncing match history and training statistics.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Aggregated usage and diagnostic telemetry.

Google's privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising & Tracking

The current version of the App does not display third-party advertisements and does not collect or track the Android Advertising ID (AAID).

5. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between the App and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. Access to user records is protected by authenticated Firestore security rules.

6. User Data Deletion & Rights

You have the right to access, update, or permanently delete your personal information at any time:

  • Local Data: You can clear all offline game and training data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device's Android settings.
  • Cloud Account & Data Deletion: You can request immediate deletion of your Firebase account and all synchronized game records by emailing us at gamewingssupport@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process and confirm your request within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at gamewingssupport@gmail.com and we will promptly delete such information.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
